How we work

Five stages. No black boxes.

A written scope, fixed milestones and regular demos. Keep scrolling; each stage stacks on the last.

Stage 01

Discover

Understand the problem, constraints and users. We produce a written scope before anything is built.

  • Your goals and current setup
  • The people who will use it
  • Content, data and accounts we will need
Stage 02

Design

Architecture, wireframes and a milestone plan, approved before build begins.

  • Written scope and staged pricing
  • Every screen designed before it is built
  • Fixed review points
Stage 03

Build

Iterative development with regular demos, so you see progress throughout. Nothing disappears into a black box.

  • Regular working reviews
  • Testing against the agreed scope
  • Decisions logged as we go
Stage 04

Deploy

Staged rollout, security review and performance testing before go-live.

  • Staged rollout with a rollback plan
  • Security and accessibility review
  • Performance testing on real devices
Stage 05

Support

Documentation, training and an agreed support plan after go-live. Source code and accounts in your name.

  • Documentation, accounts and code handed over
  • Training for your team
  • Support and response times in writing
